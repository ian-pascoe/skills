`internal-grilling` and `subagent-grilling` (and the `grill-yourself` and `grill-subagent` wrappers) now settle a load-bearing decision that rests on a public fact, like a library API, a vendor limit, or a spec, with mattpocock's `research` skill, and the ledger links the findings file it writes into your repo. Install `research` alongside `grilling`: `npx skills@latest add mattpocock/skills --skill research`.
